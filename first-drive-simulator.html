Your controls

Automatic is the default car. Switch to a manual car with a clutch in Settings.

GAS
Hold to speed up. It presses in gradually like a real pedal.
BRAKE
Hold to slow down or stay still. The longer you hold, the harder it brakes.
CLUTCH
Manual only. Touch it to press it fully down, then slide your finger UP to let it out slowly. The amber band is the biting point. Lifting your finger drops the clutch at once.
WHEEL
Drag in a circle to steer. It drifts back to centre when you let go.
◀ ▶
Turn signals, beside the wheel like real stalks. They switch off after the turn.
GEARS
Automatic: P R N D. Manual: R N 1–5, always with the clutch pressed.
START
Engine on or off. Hold the brake (and clutch in a manual).
MIRRORS
Tap a mirror to check it. Check before you signal: Mirror, Signal, Manoeuvre.
LIGHT
AUTO switches the headlights on by themselves at night and in the rain. Tap to cycle AUTO, ON, OFF.
WIPER
Tap to cycle OFF, SLOW, FAST. It pulses when it is raining and the wipers are off.
SOUND
Turns engine, tyre and rain sounds on or off. On iPhone, also check the silent switch.
